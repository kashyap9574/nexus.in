A short supply chain, run by people who answer the phone.

Nexus Fresh Export works out of Surat, an hour from the growing belts of south Gujarat and half a day from Nashik. That distance matters: less time on a truck means more shelf life at the other end.

Every consignment is graded and packed to your spec, inspected before it enters the container, and pre-cooled so the cold chain starts before the doors close. We handle the phytosanitary certificate, customs and bill of lading, and you get photos of the actual load before it sails.

Graded before it is packed

Size, colour and firmness sorted by hand, rejects removed at the shed rather than at your warehouse.

Cold chain from shed to ship

Pre-cooled and loaded into reefers set to the right temperature for each crop, not a single default setting.

Paperwork that clears

APEDA registration, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin and health documents prepared in-house.

One person on your account

The same contact from enquiry to delivery, reachable on WhatsApp in Gulf business hours.

Order to arrival

Six steps, about ten days.

Enquiry and quote

Send the product, grade, packing and destination. You get a CIF or FOB quotation the same working day, valid for the week.

Order confirmed

Proforma invoice issued. Advance payment or an irrevocable letter of credit, whichever suits your bank.

Sourcing and grading

We buy against your confirmed order, never from old stock. Sorted by size and colour, damaged pieces removed at the shed.

Packing and pre-cooling

Packed in your branded or plain material, then brought down to carrying temperature before the container arrives.

Inspection and stuffing

Phytosanitary inspection, container stuffed and sealed. Photographs and the seal number sent to you before the truck leaves.

Documents and sailing

Bill of lading, certificate of origin and health certificate couriered and emailed. Vessel tracking shared until discharge.
